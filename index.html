Your phone and your contacts. Private.

Contacts is a full phone app for Android: Favorites, Recents with your missed calls, your contacts from A to Z, a keypad that finds people by name, and its own incoming call and in-call screens. Sign in to sync your contacts between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. Or don't: it works without an account.

Everything a phone app should do

Favorites

The people you call most, as big tiles: tap to call, press and hold for their details. Below them, the numbers you call often, taken from your call log.

Recents

Your call log by day, with missed calls in red and a count on the tab. Repeat calls from one number fold into one row. Filter to Missed only, call back with a tap, or open the history, block the number or add it to your contacts.

Contacts, A to Z

Search, a card for you on top, and a letter strip to jump through a long list. Add several numbers and emails per person, with an address, birthday, notes and a photo from your gallery.

A keypad that knows names

Type digits and matching contacts and recent calls appear above the keys. Hold 0 for +, hold 1 for voicemail, paste a number, and pick the SIM on a dual-SIM phone.

Its own call screens

Incoming calls take the whole screen, even when the phone is locked. Answer, decline, or reply with a message. In a call: mute, keypad, speaker or Bluetooth, hold, add a call, and swap or merge two calls.

Bring your contacts along

Import and export vCard (.vcf) files, share a contact, and, if you like, move the contacts already on your phone into Contacts. You are asked before anything is removed.

Sync if you want to. Private either way.

  • No account needed. Choose "Continue as guest" and everything works on your phone. Sign in later and your contacts move into your account.
  • End-to-end encrypted sync. With a Klyra account, your contacts are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, so the server only ever stores scrambled data. Your password never leaves your device. One Klyra account works in every Klyra app.
  • Still the phone's address book. Contacts keeps your contacts in Android's own address book, so other apps, such as your messaging apps, can still see them.
  • Stays on your phone. Your call log, your blocked numbers and contact photos are not synced.
  • One recovery key. You get it when you create an account. Keep it safe: without your password or that key, nobody can restore your account, not even us.

The default phone app

To handle your calls, Contacts asks to become your default phone app. You can say no and still use your contacts and place calls from the app, but incoming calls then keep the system's own screen.
