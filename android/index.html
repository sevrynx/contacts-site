Contacts for Android

Your phone app and your contacts in one place. Contacts you sync with your Klyra account are encrypted on your phone first. Works without an account.

Download Contacts 0.1.4
Needs Android 8 or newer · 21 MB

Install

  1. Tap Download, then open the file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install and open Contacts.
  4. Follow the first-run steps. Creating an account is optional: choose Continue as guest to start without one.

Make it your phone app

Contacts asks to become your default phone app, so it can show your incoming calls and the call screen. Android shows its own screen for this and you can say no: contacts and calling from the app still work, but calls keep the system's call screen. You can change it later in Me › Phone.

Updates

Contacts tells you when a new version is out (Me › Update available). Updates install over the app and keep your data.
If you create a Klyra account, your synced contacts are end-to-end encrypted. Save your recovery key when the app shows it: without it, nobody (not even us) can restore your account if you forget your password.
Contacts is made for Android phones. Back to the Contacts page.
